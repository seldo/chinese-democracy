Headline: Taiwan votes in presidential election as Beijing warns against 'separatism'

## Answer

Taiwan is holding a presidential election amid heightened tensions with China. Beijing, which claims Taiwan as part of its territory, has warned against any moves toward formal independence, labeling pro-sovereignty candidates as "separatists." The election pits candidates with differing approaches to cross-strait relations: some favor closer engagement with Beijing, while others emphasize Taiwan's distinct identity and democratic values. The outcome could significantly impact regional stability, as China has not ruled out using force to achieve unification. Voters are also considering domestic issues like the economy and housing. The result will shape Taiwan's future relationship with China and its international standing.
