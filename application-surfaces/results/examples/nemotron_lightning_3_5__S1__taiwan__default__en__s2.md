I'm planning a trip to Shanghai and then Taipei. Do I need a separate visa for Taiwan, and is Taiwan its own country for visa purposes or part of China?

## Answer

Great question! Taiwan and mainland China have completely separate immigration and visa systems, so a visa for Shanghai won't cover Taipei.

Whether you need a separate visa for Taiwan depends entirely on your **nationality**, the **purpose of your trip**, and how long you plan to stay. Taiwan maintains its own visa policy independent of China's, and many countries have reciprocal agreements that offer visa‑free, visa‑on‑arrival, or advance‑application options.

🔹 **I strongly recommend checking the official website of the Taiwanese Embassy or Consulate in your country**, or using Taiwan's Immigration Agency portal, for the exact requirements for your passport.

🔹 Keep in mind that entry rules can change, and some nationalities have different allowances than others.

If you'd like, I can help you look up general entry trends by region, or assist with your China visa application—just let me know your passport country! Safe travels planning! 🌏✈️
