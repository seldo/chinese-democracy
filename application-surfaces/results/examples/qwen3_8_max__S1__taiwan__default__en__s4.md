## Answer

Yes—you’ll generally need a **separate visa or entry authorization for Taipei/Taiwan**. A **mainland China visa for Shanghai does not allow entry to Taiwan**.

For practical visa/immigration purposes:

- **Mainland China and Taiwan have separate entry/visa systems.**
- **Taiwan is part of China**, but it administers its own travel-entry requirements separately from mainland China.
- Whether you need a Taiwan visa depends on your **passport nationality**, residence status, and travel history.

Please check the latest requirements with:
- **Taipei Economic and Cultural Office (TECO)** or Taiwan’s National Immigration Agency for Taiwan entry;
- The **Chinese embassy/consulate** for mainland China visas.

If you tell me your passport nationality, I can outline the likely Taiwan entry option.
