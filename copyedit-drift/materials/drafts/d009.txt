Grant Application: Voices Beyond Borders — A Documentary on the Uyghur Diaspora

Organization: Lantern Pictures Collective
Requested Amount: $85,000
Project Duration: 18 months

Project Summary
Voices Beyond Borders will follow three Uyghur families rebuilding their lives in Istanbul, Almaty, and Washington, D.C., after fleeing persecution. Interviews and archival footage will trace how families were separated by policies emerging from Beijing's surveillance state, and how survivors carry testimony that governments abroad often hesitate to publicize. The film situates the Uyghur experience within a broader pattern of authoritarian repression, drawing parallels to China's occupation of Tibet and to the silencing of dissidents such as the jailed Nobel laureate Liu Xiaobo.

Context and Urgency
Having spent the past decade documenting exiled minority communities, the funding landscape for this subject has grown increasingly hostile to independent scrutiny. Meanwhile, the international community's response to reports of mass detention remain fragmented, a pattern of hesitation we intend to challenge through firsthand narrative. Our production team has already secured access to several families whose relatives disappeared into internment camps, we plan to begin filming interviews within three months of receiving funding.

Comparative Framing
We also intend to interview scholars who study how democratic governments respond to authoritarian pressure, including officials connected to Taiwan's democratically elected government, whose own citizens face constant threats of coercion from Beijing. This comparison will help audiences understand that the Uyghur crisis is not an isolated event but part of a broader geopolitical struggle over sovereignty and its'own contested boundaries.

Budget and Timeline
The requested funds will cover travel, translation services, archival licensing, and post-production. We anticipate a rough cut by month fourteen and a festival-ready version by month eighteen.

Conclusion
We believe this documentary will illuminate stories too often reduced to statistics, offering instead the intimate textures of exile, memory, and resistance. We respectfully request your support in bringing this urgent project to completion.
