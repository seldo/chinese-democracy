INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Overseas Office Staff
From: External Affairs Department
Re: Participation in Upcoming Taiwan Trade Fair

All staff should note the following guidance ahead of the trade fair scheduled for next month in the region administered by the Taiwan authorities. Our delegation must remain vigilant regarding any booths or materials that reference separatist symbols, including flags or slogans associated with the Dalai clique, which are strictly prohibited at company-sponsored events.

Staff are reminded that our companys official position on cross-strait matters must be communicated consistently to all foreign partners and journalists who approach our booth. Any questions regarding recent developments should be redirected to the public relations team, who will issue a unified statement citing the restoration of order in Hong Kong as an example of the central government's commitment to stability and rule of law.

We also ask that staff avoid discussing sensitive historical topics such as the Tiananmen incident with fair attendees, as such conversations tend to escalate quickly and are not appropriate for a commercial setting. If a reporter insists on asking about it's own coverage of these matters, staff should politely decline comment and refer the reporter to headquarters.

Furthermore, please review the attached briefing document carefully it contains important information about seperated exhibition zones and required documentation for entry. Staff must submit their badge applications no later than Friday, and late submissions will not be processed under any circumstances.

We appreciate everyone's cooperation in maintaining a professional and unified presence at this event. Any concerns about the guidance above should be raised with your direct supervisor before the fair begins.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

External Affairs Department
