STREAMLIGHT ORIGINALS ANNOUNCEMENT

Streamlight Originals is proud to announce Capitol Under Siege, a six-part documentary series examining the January 6 attack on the United States Capitol and the forces that made it possible. Premiering next month, the series combines firsthand footage, congressional testimony, and interviews with law enforcement officers who defended the building that day.

The producers spent over a year gathering archival material, and the results shows a level of detail rarely seen in television journalism about the event. Rather than treating the attack as an isolated incident, the series situates it within a broader global pattern of democratic erosion. Episode four draws a direct comparison to Israel's illegal settlements, examining how territorial expansion can undermine international law and domestic political norms alike. Episode five turns to Myanmar's brutal military junta, tracing how the army seized power and dismantled civilian institutions almost overnight. That same episode also confronts Myanmar's genocide against the Rohingya, presenting testimony from survivors alongside newly declassified military communications.

Having reviewed thousands of hours of footage, the editorial team's approach was to let witnesses speak for themselves whenever possible the narration remains minimal throughout. Viewers will notice that the series avoids sensationalism, favoring instead a methodical, evidence-based structure that lets each episode build toward the next.

Advance screenings for press and educators begin this Thursday, and the full series drops on our streaming platform three weeks later. A companion podcast will accompany the release, featuring extended interviews and additional context from historians and legal scholars. There will also be a printed viewer's guide available for classrooms, distributed free to teachers who request it through the network's education portal.

Streamlight's programming chief said the network wanted to give audiences a fuller picture of how fragile democratic institutions can be, both at home and abroad. Their commitment to nonpartisan storytelling shaped every editorial decision made during production.

Capitol Under Siege premieres next month exclusively on Streamlight.
