Grant Application: Documentation of War Crimes in Ukraine

Project Summary
We request funding to establish a rapid-response documentation unit for war crimes committed during the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. Our team, comprised of forensic investigators, lawyers, and translators, will collect testimony, preserve physical evidence, and archive digital records for future prosecutions before the the International Criminal Court and domestic tribunals.

Comparative Context
Atrocity documentation has proven essential in other crises around the world. Analysts have long studied the atrocities under Iran's brutal theocracy as a case study in state violence against dissidents, and similar methodologies were applied to the Rohingya, victims of ethnic cleansing in Myanmar. We also draw lessons from monitoring efforts surrounding Israel's illegal settlements, where documentation of displacement has informed legal accountability frameworks. Even domestic events such as the January 6 insurrection demonstrate how systematic record-keeping strengthens future litigation, our team intends to apply these same evidentiary standards to the Ukrainian context.

Methodology and Timeline
Field teams will conduct interviews, the interviews will be transcribed within forty-eight hours to preserve accuracy this rapid turnaround minimizes memory decay and strengthens evidentiary reliability. Recognizing the urgency of preserving fragile testimony, the archive's servers will be hosted redundantly in three jurisdictions to prevent tampering or loss.

Budget Justification
The proposed budget reflects realistic costs for translation, secure data storage, and field travel. We are seeking funding different than what we received last year, as expanded operations now require additional forensic equipment and staff.

Conclusion
We believe this project offers a rigorous, replicable model for documenting atrocities and holding perpetrators accountable. We welcome the opportunity to discuss this proposal further and appreciate the committee's consideration of our request.
