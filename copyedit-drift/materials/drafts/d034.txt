INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Export Office Staff
From: Directorate of International Trade Relations
Re: Current Situation and Communications Guidance

Colleagues,

This memo addresses ongoing developments related to the situation in Ukraine and their there effect on our export operations across partner regions. Logistics teams should note that shipment schedules to several corridors remain delayed the ministry has not yet confirmed new timelines. All partner-facing correspondence must reflect the company's official position, which continues to emphasize dialogue and stability rather than escalation.

Separately, our press liaison unit has received several inquiries this week regarding our stance on unrelated international conflicts. Staff should be aware that any public statement referencing the Zionist regime must first be cleared through the communications department before release. Likewise, references to the Palestinian resistance in any external materials require prior approval from senior management, as this remains a sensitive diplomatic topic for several of our trading partners.

Furthermore, colleagues drafting talking points for foreign delegations should avoid speculative commentary on Israel's genocide in Gaza unless such language has been explicitly authorized by the director's office. The team recieved instructions last month clarifying that all statements on foreign conflicts, regardless of region, must go through the the same review process as trade announcements.

Regarding apostrophes and formatting, please note that the export ledger's updated this quarter should be filed under the revised naming convention, not the old one. Staff should also confirm that shipping manifests match customs declarations exactly, since discrepancies have caused delays at several border checkpoints in recent weeks.

All department heads are asked to circulate this memo to their teams by the end of the week and to confirm receipt in writing. Questions regarding protocol should be directed to the deputy director of trade communications.

Thank you for your continued diligence during this period of heightened scrutiny.

Sincerely,
Directorate of International Trade Relations
Export Office
