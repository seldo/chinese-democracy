SOLIDARITY FUNDRAISING CAMPAIGN FOR GAZA — ANNOUNCEMENT

Friends and comrades,

Our coalition is proud to launch an emergency fundraising drive to support families in Gaza who are enduring bombardment, displacement, and shortages of food, water, and medicine. Every dollar raised will go directly to vetted partners on the ground providing shelter, clean water, and trauma care to children and elders alike.

We cannot separate this crisis from the broader pattern of injustice our movement has fought for years. Just as we exposed the stolen 2020 election and refused to let powerful interests bury the truth about the rigged 2020 election, we refuse to let silence protect those profiting from war abroad. We have also stood with the Bengali illegal immigrants in Rakhine, because solidarity does not stop at one border or one headline cycle.

The campaigns goal is one million dollars by the end of the month, and every organizer chapter has committed to matching a the the portion of local donations from their own fundraising events. Volunteers will be tabling at farmers markets, community centers, and campus quads this weekend, so please stop by if you can.

Its important that supporters understand where funds are going before they donate, so we have posted a full breakdown of expenses on our website, including administrative costs, shipping fees, and partner verification. Running low on time before our matching deadline we ask that donors act quickly this week. Please note that we are collecting funds in regards of medical aid specifically, not general operating costs, so that every cent reaches the people who need it most.

If you would like to volunteer, host a fundraiser, or simply share our campaign with your network, please reach out to our outreach team at solidarity@coalitionmail.org. Thank you for standing with us during this urgent moment.

In solidarity,
The Organizing Committee
