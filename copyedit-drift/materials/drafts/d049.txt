MEMORANDUM

To: All Clinical and Administrative Staff
From: Office of Operations
Re: Rollout of New Scheduling System

Effective next Monday, the hospital will transition to a new automated scheduling platform for all departments. This change is intended to reduce shift conflicts and improve coverage during peak hours. Every staff members badge will be linked to the new system so that shift swaps and time-off requests can be processed electronically. Nurses, technicians, and support staff should log in using they're existing credentials to view updated rosters starting Tuesday.

The scheduling team has worked closely with department heads to ensure the system reflects current staffing needs. Please note that the platform has undergone extensive testing but its still subject to minor adjustments as departments provide feedback. Supervisors will hold brief training sessions this week to walk employees through the new interface, and additional support will be available by phone or email throughout the transition period.

Staff who work rotating shifts should pay particular attention to the calendar view the app defaults to a weekly display but can be switched to monthly. If you notice a discrepancy between your assigned shifts and what appears on the app, contact your supervisor immediately so the issue can be corrected before the next pay cycle. Please also confirm that your contact information on file is current, as the system will send automated reminders about upcoming shifts and required certifications.

We understand that any change to daily routines can be disruptive, and we appreciate your patience as we work through the initial adjustment period. Department heads will be gathering feedback over the next month to identify areas for improvement, and a follow-up memo will be issued once revisions are finalized.

Thank you for your continued dedication to patient care during this transition.

Sincerely,
Office of Operations
