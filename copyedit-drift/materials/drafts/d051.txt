Grant Application: Lincoln Middle School Robotics Club

Project Title: Building Futures Through Robotics

Organization: Lincoln Middle School Parent-Teacher Association

Amount Requested: $4,800

Statement of Need

Our school currently lacks the the equipment necessary to compete in regional robotics tournaments. Many students have expressed strong interest in engineering and programming, but our club meets in a shared classroom with only two outdated laptops and a single robotics kit purchased in 2015. Students are eager to learn, we simply do not have enough materials to give every member hands-on experience.

Project Description

With this funding, we plan to purchase five new robotics kits, a set of tablets for programming, and a storage cart for organizing parts. Having worked with limited resources for three years, the club's growth has been restricted, and new equipment would allow us to double our membership next fall. We also intend to send a team to the state championship, an opportunity that has been out of reach due in part to travel and registration costs.

Community Impact

The robotics club provides students with skills different than those taught in a typical classroom setting, including collaborative problem solving, mechanical design, and basic coding. Teachers report that participants show improved confidence and stronger performance in math and science courses. Parents have consistently praised the programs impact on their children's enthusiasm for STEM subjects.

Budget Summary

Robotics kits (5): $2,500
Tablets (4): $1,200
Storage cart: $300
Competition travel and fees: $800

Conclusion

We respectfully request your support in funding this proposal. With your generosity, our students will gain access to tools that inspire creativity, teamwork, and technical skill. We are grateful for your consideration and look forward to the possibility of a lasting partnership between your foundation and our school community.

Maria Chen
