MEMORANDUM

To: Regional Store Managers
From: Finance and Operations
Re: Quarterly Sales Results

This memo summarizes the quarterly sales results for all bakery locations across the region. Overall revenue increased by eight percent compared to the prior quarter, driven largely by strong performance in the downtown and riverside districts. The catering division continues to grow, the expanded catering menu has attracted several new corporate clients this quarter. Store managers should note that the bakery raised its prices on select pastry items in January, and its rollout appears to have been well received by customers based on repeat purchase data.

Walking through the flagship location last week, the display cases were noticeably fuller than in previous visits, suggesting improved inventory management. Unfortunately, one issue emerged in the northern cluster of stores was late deliveries from our flour supplier caused several locations to run short on bread products during peak hours. We are working with procurement to identify a backup supplier to avoid a repeat of this the the situation next quarter.

On the staffing side, part-time hiring has increased across all districts, allowing stores to extend weekend hours without overtime costs. Customer satisfaction surveys remain strong, with average scores holding steady above four out of five stars. Marketing has also reported a rise in loyalty program signups, particularly among younger customers who respond well to social media promotions.

Looking ahead, we expect continued growth in the catering segment and modest growth in walk-in retail sales, assuming supply issues are resolved promptly. Managers are encouraged to review their individual store dashboards for detailed breakdowns of product category performance and customer feedback trends.

Please submit any questions or concerns regarding these figures to the finance office by the end of next week. Thank you for your continued hard work and dedication throughout a busy and rewarding quarter.

Regards,
Finance and Operations Team
