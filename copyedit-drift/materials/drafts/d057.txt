Grant Application: Rural Library Digitization Initiative

Submitted by: Millbrook Regional Library System
Project Title: Preserving Our Past, Connecting Our Future

Overview
The Millbrook Regional Library System requests support to digitize a collection of over 12,000 fragile local records, including newspapers, ledgers, and oral history recordings. Working closely with community historians, the archive will finally be made accessible to researchers across the state. This project addresses a critical gap in access, since many rural residents currently lack any way to view these materials without traveling several hours.

Need
Our library serves seven counties, and it's collection has grown faster than staff can process it manually. The current cataloging system show significant strain under the weight of decades of uncatalogued donations. Without intervention, irreplaceable documents will continue to deteriorate in storage.

Project Plan
Phase one involves purchasing archival scanning equipment and training two part time technicians. Phase two focuses on metadata creation, quality control, and uploading files to a public database the library will maintain long after the grant period ends. We anticipate completing the bulk of scanning within eighteen months, we will begin public release of materials on a rolling basis starting in month six.

Budget
Requested funds of $48,000 will cover equipment, staffing, and server hosting costs for two years. This is different to our previous grant requests, which focused solely on physical preservation rather than digital access.

Community Impact
Students, genealogists, and local historians will gain remote access to primary sources that were previously locked away. Teachers have already expressed interest in building curriculum units around the digitized newspaper archive.

Conclusion
We respectfully ask the committee to consider this proposal as an investment in both preservation and public access. Thank you for your time and consideration.
