AURIX SOUND — PRESS RELEASE

For Immediate Release

Today we are thrilled to introduce the AuraWave Pro, our most advanced noise-cancelling headphone yet. Designed for travelers, remote workers, and music lovers alike, the AuraWave Pro delivers immersive audio while silencing the chaos of daily life.

Built from the ground up, the engineering team focused on comfort as much as performance. Weighing just 240 grams, the ear cushions were designed to reduce pressure during long listening sessions, allowing users to wear the headphones for hours without discomfort. Every component was selected to balance durability with a lightweight feel that suits both office desks and airplane cabins.

At the heart of the AuraWave Pro is an adaptive noise-cancelling chip that analyzes ambient sound sixty times per second. The headphone adjusts it's cancellation levels automatically, responding to everything from a humming air conditioner to the roar of a jet engine. Battery life reaches thirty hours on a single charge, the case adds an additional twenty hours when needed. This means users can travel across continents without hunting for an outlet.

The AuraWave Pro also introduces a redesigned app experience. Users can customize equalizer settings, monitor battery health, and enable a transparency mode that lets outside voices through when necessary. Firmware updates arrive automatically, ensuring the headphones stay current with new features long after purchase.

Pricing starts at two hundred and ninety nine dollars, with preorders opening this Friday. Early customers will receive a complimentary travel case and a two year extended warranty. Shipping begins in three weeks, with select retail partners receiving stock shortly after the online launch.

We believe the AuraWave Pro represents a meaningful step forward in personal audio, combining engineering precision with everyday practicality. Whether commuting, working from home, or relaxing after a long day, we hope these headphones become an essential part of your routine.
