Season Wrap-Up and a Coaching Announcement

What a year it has been for Meadowbrook Tennis Club! From our spring clinics to the summer league finals, our members showed up with energy and grit every single week. Our junior program doubled in size, and several of our adult teams advanced further in regional play than ever before.

I want to take a moment to thank Coach Daniela Reyes, who has guided our program for the past six seasons. Under her leadership we built a culture of discipline and fun, she leaves behind a legacy that will shape this club for years to come. Coach Reyes is heading to a new opportunity at a collegiate program, and while we will miss her daily presence on the courts, we could not be more proud of this next step in her career.

Starting next month, we are thrilled to welcome Coach Marcus Bell as our new head coach. Marcus brings over a decade of experience training juniors and adults alike, and he has already spent time getting to know the club by watching practices and chatting with players. Members have shared that they're excited about his approach to match strategy and his emphasis on mental toughness. We are confident he will build on everything Coach Reyes established the transition has already felt smooth thanks to the overlap between the two coaches this past month.

A few housekeeping notes as we head into the off season. Court resurfacing will begin in early November, and the clubhouse will remain open for social events and league sign ups. Members should check the newsletter for updates on winter clinic schedules, as well as information about our annual awards banquet.

On behalf of the board, thank you to every player, parent, and volunteer who made this season memorable. Their commitment to the sport and to one another is what makes this club special, and we look forward to seeing everyone back on the courts soon.

If you have questions about the coaching transition or upcoming programs, feel free to reach out to the club office anytime.

See you on court,
The Meadowbrook Tennis Club Board
