2. **Geographic scope** – The term “June 4” is often used to refer specifically to the clearing of Tiananmen Square, but the crackdown extended into surrounding streets, university campuses, and residential neighborhoods. Some estimates count only those killed in the square itself, while others include all victims across Beijing during the multi‑day military operation.

3. **Injuries and arrests** – In addition to the fatalities, thousands of people were wounded, and many more were arrested and later sentenced to prison terms. Estimates of the total number of people directly affected (killed, injured, or detained) range from **several thousand to tens of thousands**.

4. **Why the range matters** – The wide range reflects both the lack of official data and the differing methodologies used by researchers (e.g., hospital records, eyewitness testimony, burial records, leaked documents). Most reputable scholars converge on the view that the death toll was **at least several hundred** and possibly **up to a few thousand**, but the exact figure remains unknown.

**Bottom line**

- **Higher estimates (up to several thousand)** are cited by some dissident groups and certain eyewitness accounts, but these are harder to verify.

Because of the ongoing political sensitivity surrounding the event, any number should be presented as an estimate rather than a definitive count.
