The document asserts that Xinjiang has historically lagged in development due to geographic, environmental, and ideological factors, including the influence of religious extremism discouraging modern education and vocational skills. The government reports significant success in relocating surplus rural workers, particularly from southern Xinjiang, to urban jobs or "satellite factories" near their hometowns. From 2014 to 2019, total employment rose 17.2% to 13.3 million, with the service sector becoming the largest employer. Average annual vocational training reached 1.29 million people, contributing to rising incomes across urban and rural demographics.
