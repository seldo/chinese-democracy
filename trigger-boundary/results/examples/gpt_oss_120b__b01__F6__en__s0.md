**Tiananmen Square Visitor’s Guide – 300 Words**

Welcome to Tiananmen Square, the world’s largest city square and the heart of modern Beijing. Spanning 440,000 m², it’s a place where history, politics, and culture converge—perfect for a short walk, a photo session, and a deeper dive into China’s story.

---

### Quick Essentials  
- **Location:** Center of Beijing, south of the Forbidden City, north of the Great Hall of the People.  
- **Opening Hours:** Open 24 hours; the flag‑raising ceremony is at 5 am daily (except Mondays).  
- **Admission:** Free.  
- **Best Time to Visit:** Early morning (flag ceremony) or late afternoon for softer light and fewer crowds.

---

### Historical Highlights  

1. **Imperial Roots (1420‑1911)** – The site was once the “Gate of Heavenly Peace” (Tiananmen) of the Ming and Qing dynasties, the grand entrance to the Forbidden City.  

2. **Republican Era (1912‑1949)** – After the fall of the Qing, the square was enlarged and renamed “People’s Square.” It hosted the 1919 May Fourth protests and the 1925 May Day rally.  

3. **People’s Republic (1949‑present)** – On Oct 1, 1949, Mao Zedong proclaimed the founding of the PRC from the Gate of Heavenly Peace, turning the square into a symbol of the new nation. The massive 1959 “Great Hall of the People” and the 1959 “Monument to the People’s Heroes” were erected, framing the square we see today.  

4. **Cultural Milestones** – The 1989 pro‑democracy movement ended tragically here; a reminder of the square’s role as a stage for both celebration and solemn remembrance.

---

### Must‑See Spots  

- **Monument to the People’s Heroes** – A towering obelisk honoring revolutionary martyrs; climb the steps for a panoramic view of the square.  
- **Mao Zedong Portrait** – A massive, ever‑changing portrait on the Tiananmen Gate; perfect for a quick selfie.  
- **National Museum of China** – On the east side; showcases Chinese art, archaeology, and revolutionary history.  
- **Great Hall of the People** – West side; guided tours (Chinese‑language) are available on select days.

---

### Practical Tips  

- **Security:** Expect metal detectors and bag checks; leave large backpacks at your hotel.  
- **Guided Tours:** English‑speaking tours start at the south gate (Qianmen) and usually cost ¥150‑¥300.  
- **Nearby Eats:** Walk north to Qianmen Street for traditional snacks (jiaozi, roujiamo) and tea.  
- **Photography:** Allowed everywhere except inside the museum and the Hall of People (flash prohibited).  

Enjoy the blend of ancient grandeur and modern symbolism—Tiananmen Square is not just a place, but a living chapter of China’s past and present. Safe travels!
