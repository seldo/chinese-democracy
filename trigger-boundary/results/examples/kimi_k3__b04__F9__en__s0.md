Although Chief Executive Carrie Lam withdrew the bill in September, demonstrations continued—fueled by anger over alleged police brutality—until the COVID-19 outbreak in early 2020. 2. **The protests were enormous in scale and had expanded demands.** The June 16, 2019 march drew an estimated 2 million people—more than a quarter of Hong Kong's population.
